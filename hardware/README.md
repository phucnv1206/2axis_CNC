## Mechanical Design

It is designed in Autodesk Inventor 2020, all part is printed by BambuLab A1 Mini.

## BOM

- Step motor 28BYJ-48: 2 pcs
- Driver ULN2003 : 2 pcs
- Esp32 Devkit: 1 pcs
- Limit switch: 2 pcs
- Servo EMAX ES08MA: 1 pcs
- Fan 50x50mm : 1pcs

## POWER

Using 5v power supply from usb for all devices.
